#+TITLE: Wavelet plugin
#+OPTIONS: toc:nil num:t

Implementation: [[file:../../spiny/plugins/wavelet/extractor.py]], which relies on the optional
=wavelet_prosody_toolkit=.

The plugin shows the continuous wavelet transform of a prosodic contour, on frames of 5 ms:

1. *Energy*: RMS energy in the 200–5000 Hz band, then smoothed by peak smoothing.
2. *F0*: extracted between the minimum and the maximum F0 with the =inst_freq= tracker, then post-processed. If the
   post-processing fails (no voiced frame, for instance), the raw F0 is used.
3. *Combination* of the two contours, on their common frames:
   #+begin_example
   sum:      normalize_std(F0) × weight_F0 + normalize_std(energy) × weight_energy
   product:  (normalize_minmax(F0) + weight_F0) × (normalize_minmax(energy) + weight_energy)
   #+end_example
   In a product, a null weight or a flat contour contributes 1.
4. *Detrending* (=remove_bias=, 800) and normalization.
5. *Wavelet transform*: Mexican hat, period 3, first frequency 32, 34 scales spaced by 0.25. The real part is displayed.

The analysis covers the whole signal and runs when the plugin is selected or "Extract" is pressed.
